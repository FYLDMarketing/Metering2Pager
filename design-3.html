The problem

Install failures start before the team arrives on site

AMP8 programmes are moving from meter supply to outcome-based contracts, where one partner owns the customer, the install and the data. Under that model, every aborted visit is your cost and your service level. Quality assurance and predictable outcomes are now directly part of securing contracts.

Incorrect or incomplete job information is fast becoming the #1 cause of aborted meter installs. At programme scale, even a small abort rate hits C-MeX and adds significant cost before anyone knows there's a problem.
