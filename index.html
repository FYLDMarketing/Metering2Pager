The problem

Install failures start before the crew arrives on site

AMP8 programmes are moving from meter supply to outcome-based contracts, where one partner owns the customer, the install and the data. Under that model, every aborted visit is your cost and your service level.

Incorrect or incomplete job information is fast becoming the #1 cause of aborted meter installs. At programme scale, even a small abort rate hits C-MeX and adds significant cost before anyone knows there's a problem.

The Triage Agent: what it checks before anyone leaves the depot

New installs and end-of-life replacements fail for the same reasons. The agent catches them from the customer's video.

01

Meter location and job type

Internal or external? Flags jobs that need a dig, so excavation is booked before the first visit.

02

Working space around the meter

Customers say there's room when there isn't. Measures the clearance from video.

03

Pipe material and size

Lead, copper or plastic, and what diameter. The van leaves with the right fittings.

04

Access obstructions

Boxes, mobility aids, locked cupboards. Customers don't flag these. The agent spots them upfront.

05

Stop tap presence and condition

Too technical to ask directly. Finds the stop tap on video and flags a seized or missing one before it aborts the job.

AI-assisted triage, not automated decisions. The agent surfaces analysis. Your reviewer makes the call. Human accountability is retained throughout.

  • Your client's data, ready to hand over: structured job records that integrate with existing systems
